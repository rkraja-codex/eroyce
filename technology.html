BATTERY TECHNOLOGY

Why LFP is the Right Choice.

🔋

Safest Chemistry

LFP cells are highly stable — no thermal runaway risk that plagues NMC batteries. Critical for vehicles operating in India's hot, humid climate.

⚡

Long Cycle Life

LFP batteries support 2,000+ charge cycles — significantly longer than alternative chemistries. Reduces long-term battery replacement costs for fleet operators.

🌡️

Thermal Stability

Operates reliably across a wide temperature range — designed for India's tropical ambient conditions without requiring active thermal management.
