About E-Royce Motors

E Royce Motors India Pvt. Ltd. — electric vehicle manufacturer from Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, pioneering clean transportation since 2017.

OUR STORY

Pioneering Clean Transportation.

E Royce Motors India Pvt. Ltd. is an electric two-wheeler and three-wheeler manufacturer headquartered in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Founded with a singular vision — to accelerate India's transition to sustainable, zero-emission mobility — we design, manufacture, and distribute high-performance EVs built specifically for Indian road conditions.

Our manufacturing footprint spans Plant I in Kinathukadavu, Coimbatore (Tamil Nadu) and Plant II in Raipur, Chhattisgarh — producing a diverse lineup of electric vehicles: from high-payload commercial cargo carriers to premium electric scooters for daily urban commuting. Every vehicle we build is engineered to withstand the demands of India's diverse road environments.

We are ISO 9001:2015 certified and all our vehicles are ARAI (Automotive Research Association of India) compliant — meeting the highest Indian automotive safety and performance standards.

OUR JOURNEY

Milestones.

2017

Company Founded

E Royce Motors India Pvt. Ltd. established in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, with a mission to manufacture world-class electric vehicles for India.

2018

First Commercial EV Launch

Launch of the eBull — E-Royce's first commercial electric cargo three-wheeler, designed for India's last-mile logistics sector.

2020

Electric 2-Wheeler Range

Expansion into electric scooters — introducing the Spike, RS90, and RS180 to address the urban commuter market across Tamil Nadu.

2022

ISO 9001:2015 Certification

E-Royce achieves ISO 9001:2015 certification — affirming our commitment to quality management and manufacturing excellence.

2024

Statewide Dealer Network

Expansion of dealer network across Tamil Nadu — with direct outlets in Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, and authorised dealers in 20+ districts.

2025

Service Network Expansion & FAME II

Strengthening our authorised service centre network across Tamil Nadu districts and aligning our vehicle lineup with FAME II & EMPS eligibility criteria for customer subsidies.

2026

National Expansion & New Models

Scaling operations beyond Tamil Nadu with new state markets, introducing next-generation EV models, and deepening our fleet solutions for B2B logistics and passenger mobility segments.

Achievements & Recognition.

Government engagements, investment milestones, and institutional partnerships marking E-Royce's growth.

Foundation Stone — Andhra Pradesh Facility

Foundation stone laid for E-Royce Motors' new facility at Ammavaripeta (Kuppam Assembly, Chittoor District, Andhra Pradesh) by Hon'ble Chief Minister Sri Nara Chandra Babu Naidu, on 28th October 2025 — attended by the Deputy CM, state ministers, MPs/MLAs, and senior IAS officials.

Chief Minister Meeting — Investment Plan

E-Royce representatives met the Hon'ble Chief Minister on 20th March 2025, presenting a two-phase investment plan: ₹50 Cr (Phase 1) and ₹150 Cr (Phase 2), projected to create 400–500 direct jobs, alongside a CSR commitment of 100 e-autos for solid waste management.

Tamil Nadu Industry Engagement

E-Royce representatives at an official industry event presided over by Hon'ble Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu, Thiru M.K. Stalin.

Academic Partnership — Sri Krishna College of Technology

E-Royce representatives with Sri Krishna College of Technology, Kovaipudur, Coimbatore, marking a formal collaboration between the two organisations.
