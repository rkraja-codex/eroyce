WHY E-ROYCE

Pioneering Clean Transportation for India.

E Royce Motors India Pvt. Ltd. is an electric two-wheeler and three-wheeler manufacturer headquartered in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. We build high-performance EVs designed for demanding Indian road conditions — delivering exceptional payload capacity, drastic operating cost savings, and zero tailpipe emissions.
