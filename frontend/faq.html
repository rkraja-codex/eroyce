Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about E-Royce vehicles, booking, and support.

What is E-Royce? ▼
E-Royce Motors India Pvt. Ltd. is a manufacturer of premium electric two-wheelers and commercial three-wheelers, based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. We focus on providing eco-friendly, accessible, and high-performance electric mobility solutions.
What types of electric vehicles does E-Royce offer? ▼
We offer electric scooters for urban commuting (Spike, RS90, RS180) and commercial electric three-wheelers (eBull for cargo, Sardar for passenger transport).
How can I book an E-Royce vehicle? ▼
You can easily book your vehicle online through our website by visiting the "Book Your Vehicle" page. Just fill out your details, select your preferred model, and submit the inquiry.
Do I need a driving license or RTO registration for the Spike scooter? ▼
No, the E-Royce Spike is a low-speed electric scooter (top speed 25 km/h) and does not require RTO registration or a driving license to operate.
Where can I find E-Royce dealers? ▼
We have a growing network across Tamil Nadu with direct outlets in Chennai, Coimbatore, and Madurai, along with authorised dealers in over 20 districts. You can find more details on our Dealers page.
How can I contact customer support? ▼
You can reach our customer support via the Contact page, or click the WhatsApp icon on the bottom right of the screen to chat with us directly.
