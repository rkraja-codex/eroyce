Privacy Policy

How we handle and protect your personal information.

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

E Royce Motors India Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website eroyce.in, use our services, or interact with our dealer network.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Submit a vehicle booking enquiry or test drive request.
  • Apply for a dealership.
  • Contact us via our forms, email, or WhatsApp.
  • Subscribe to our newsletters or updates.

This information may include your name, email address, phone number, location, and any message content you provide.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect primarily to:

  • Process and respond to your enquiries and requests.
  • Provide customer support and follow up on vehicle bookings.
  • Evaluate dealership applications.
  • Improve our website, products, and services.
  • Send administrative information and updates regarding our products.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. We may share your information with our authorised dealers and service partners solely for the purpose of fulfilling your vehicle enquiries, service requests, or dealership applications.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. However, no electronic transmission over the internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.
